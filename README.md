# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution that provides valuable insights into employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, job roles, and departmental performance. It helps HR teams identify trends, understand employee behavior, and make informed business decisions.

## Dataset used
-<a href="https://github.com/rajshreepawar59241-cyber/HR-Analytics-Dashboard/blob/main/HR_Analytics.csv">HR Dataset</a>

## 🎯 Business Problem

High employee attrition affects productivity, increases recruitment costs, and impacts overall organizational performance.

This dashboard helps answer key business questions:

- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which age groups leave the company most frequently?
- Which job roles have the highest turnover?
- Does salary influence employee attrition?
- Which education backgrounds experience the highest attrition?

# 💡 Key Insights

- Total employees are **1,470**, with **237 employees leaving**, resulting in an **attrition rate of 16.1%**.
- Employees aged **26–35 years** have the highest attrition.
- **Life Sciences** has the highest employee attrition among education backgrounds.
- **Laboratory Technicians** experience the highest attrition among job roles.
- Employees earning **up to 5K** salary show the highest turnover.
- Male employees have slightly higher attrition than female employees.
- Research & Development contributes significantly to overall attrition.

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

- Improve employee engagement for employees aged 26–35.
- Introduce retention programs for Laboratory Technicians.
- Review compensation for lower salary groups.
- Conduct employee satisfaction surveys.
- Strengthen career development and training opportunities.
